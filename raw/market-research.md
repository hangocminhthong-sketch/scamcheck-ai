# Market research notes (source material, 12 Nov 2026)

## Problem size
- GASA & Feedzai, Global State of Scams 2025 (n=46,000 adults, 42 markets): 57% of adults
  experienced a scam in the past 12 months; 23% lost money; est. US$442B lost; 73% believe they
  can identify a scam. Shopping scams most common (54% of victims), then investment and
  "unexpected money" scams (48% each). Wire/bank transfer is the top payment channel (29%).
- GASA State of Scams in Southeast Asia 2025 (n=6,000): 63% experienced a scam; US$23.6B stolen;
  Vietnam has the highest share of adults targeted (77%). People verify with weak heuristics
  ("too good to be true" 32%, checking for a phone number 30%). Only 22% recovered losses.

## Platform / publisher fit
- Apero: 1B+ downloads, Top 20 global Google Play non-game publisher. Portfolio includes
  Caller ID & Call Protect (55M+ downloads), PDF Reader (50M+), Document Scanner (10M+).
- Sensor Tower State of Mobile 2026: non-game app IAP revenue passed games for the first time;
  GenAI app revenue tripled to >$5B, downloads doubled to 3.8B.
- Google Play AI-Generated Content policy: in-app report/flag for AI output.
