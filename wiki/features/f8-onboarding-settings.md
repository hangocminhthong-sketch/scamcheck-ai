# F8 — Onboarding, language, privacy

## Behaviour
- First launch: one onboarding screen (what it does, 3 bullets, privacy note: "Messages are sent to
  our AI for checking and are not stored by us") → "Get started" → `onboarding_completed`.
- Language: auto from device (vi → Vietnamese, else English), switchable in Settings → `language_changed`.
- Settings: language, delete history, privacy policy (in-app page), Pro waitlist, app version.

## Done when
- [ ] First launch shows onboarding; second launch goes straight to Home.
- [ ] Switching to Tiếng Việt changes Home title to Vietnamese.
- [ ] Privacy policy page opens inside the app.
